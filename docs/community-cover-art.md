# Community cover illustrations
The eight Community Suite covers (Mühlenmeister, Reversi, Rhythm, Würfelrennen,
Dame-Duell, Hnefatafl, BeatBox and Soundquiz) were generated specifically for the
MuPiBox Community with OpenAI image generation, using this repository's
Kreuz & Kreis illustration as the authorized mascot/style reference.
They are supplied under the repository license. No third-party stock assets,
fonts or music were added.

Each cover is one coherent illustration with the orange headphone-wearing
axolotl, an organic wooden MuPi/title sign and a recognizable activity motif.
Runtime JPEGs use 832×650 pixels (the 416:325 coverflow ratio), with a versioned
filename to avoid stale image caches. The old geometric cover generator skips
apps whose manifest uses these curated illustrations.

Prompt pattern: one full-bleed landscape game illustration matching Kreuz &
Kreis, orange axolotl with cream belly/coral gills/black-yellow headphones;
top wooden sign with golden MuPi and cream exact app title; mascot left,
activity right; rich woodland lighting and foliage; no UI, buttons or collage.
Motifs: nine men's morris / green Reversi board / four neon rhythm lanes /
winding woodland race trail, round tokens and die / crowned checkers / Nordic tafl king and attackers /
drum-pad sequencer / listening quiz with instruments and question mark.

## Cover cleanup (Preview 26)

Würfelrennen uses an original winding race trail and finish flag, with no
cross-shaped Ludo board or four home circles. Schach, Kreuz & Kreis and
4er-Reihe have repaired full-bleed bottom edges, retaining their established
illustrations and titles. These edits were produced with OpenAI image editing.
New cover filenames prevent stale cached white stripes.
